Verify a declared scientific claim, with the evidence in view.

NatalIA checks structured formalizations of mathematics and physics. It does not read papers, simulate nature, or certify Lean proofs unless those paths are actually implemented.

  1. Verified within declared assumptions

    Every submitted obligation closed in the supported fragment. Job success is not itself a theorem.

  2. Refuted by a checked counterexample

    One independently checked witness is enough to reject a universal claim.

  3. Inconclusive or unsupported

    Invalid input, an open gap, or a fragment the laboratory does not encode.

Fast verification

SMT-relative acceptance, exact rational witnesses, and compile-time dimensions. Not an independent certificate.

Certified verification

Requires a recheckable polynomial kernel certificate. SMT is not reused as Certified.

Trust has explicit boundaries.

Fast acceptance is relative to the SMT solver. Certified mode requires an independently recheckable certificate. Cryptographic signatures and hashes do not prove mathematical truth. Proof of formalization does not prove fidelity to natural language.

Explicit Verification Capabilities

              
    
                
  • Strict JSON AST without arbitrary code execution.
